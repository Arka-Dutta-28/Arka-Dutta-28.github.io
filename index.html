M.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence · IIT Gandhinagar

I am an M.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence at IIT Gandhinagar, advised by Prof. Shanmuganathan Raman at the Computer Vision, Imaging and Graphics (CVIG) Lab. My thesis is on computer vision for surgical video, and is part of a DST-funded collaboration with NTU Singapore.

Outside the thesis I build machine learning systems and put them online. I am also a teaching assistant for the Digital Systems course.

Before this I studied Mathematics and Computing at IIT Patna. My all-India ranks were 949 in GATE Data Science and AI 2025 and 6,120 in JEE Advanced 2021.
